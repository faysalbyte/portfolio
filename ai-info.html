SEO Best Practices for Developers

As a MERN Stack Developer, I implement SEO best practices including:

  • Semantic HTML5 – Using proper heading hierarchy (h1-h6), article, section, and nav elements
  • Structured Data – JSON-LD for Person, ProfessionalService, and WebSite schemas
  • Performance Optimization – Lazy loading, optimized images, and minimal render-blocking resources
  • Accessibility – ARIA attributes, proper focus management, and screen reader support
  • Mobile Optimization – Responsive design with mobile-first approach
